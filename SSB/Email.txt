Patch-06 is a minor PFI 2026 patch related to the ZAP utility compatibility with SPN connectivity.
 
PFI 2026 supports SPN capability by default. However, the ZAP utility required an additional compatibility fix to work correctly with SPN connectivity. This fix was released as part of Patch-06.
 
We have applied Patch-06 in the BUAT Azure environment and validated the ZAP utility across all three clients. All three are working as expected with SPN connectivity and no issues has been reported so far.
 
Patch-06 contains minor fixes and does not introduce any functional changes that would put us out of sync with NTT Production. The purpose of applying it is specifically to address the ZAP/SPN compatibility issue.
